## Welcome

issues.cash is a decentralized bounty platform that connects open source maintainers with contributors.

By using issues.cash, you agree to these terms.

## How It Works

- **Maintainers** create bounties by commenting on GitHub/GitLab issues and funding a smart contract with BCH.
- **Contributors** claim bounties by submitting pull/merge requests and providing their BCH address.
- **Smart contracts** hold the funds in escrow.
- **Our oracle** verifies GitHub/GitLab events and signs transactions to release funds when a PR is merged.
- **Refunds** happen automatically when an issue is closed without a merged PR, or after the bounty expires.

## Our Role

We operate the oracle that connects GitHub/GitLab events to the blockchain. Here's what that means:

- **We don't custody your funds.** Funds are held by smart contracts on the Bitcoin Cash blockchain, not by us.
- **We don't mediate disputes.** The smart contract is the arbiter. If a PR (on GitHub) or MR (on GitLab) is merged, the contributor gets paid. If the issue is closed, the maintainer gets refunded.
- **We sign transactions.** Our oracle verifies events and authorizes payouts, but the contract enforces the rules.
- **We can't steal your funds.** The contract only allows payment to the contributor (on merge) or refund to the maintainer (on close/timeout).

## Timeout Protection

Every bounty has an expiration date (default: 90 days). After this date, the maintainer can reclaim their funds directly from the smart contract — no oracle signature required. This protects you even if our service goes offline.

## Fees

- **Commission:** We may charge a commission on successful bounty completions. The current rate (if any) is displayed when a bounty is paid. No commission is charged on refunds or timeouts.
- **Network fees:** Bitcoin Cash transaction fees are paid from the bounty amount. These are typically very small (fractions of a cent).
- **No hidden fees:** What you see is what you get.
